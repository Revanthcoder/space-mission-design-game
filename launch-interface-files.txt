 Relevant files for the launch interface flow

Project root: space-mission-design-game

1. src/components/mission/MissionLaunchPage.tsx
   Main launch control screen for mission launch setup.
   Includes countdown, mission details, readiness checklist, metrics, and launch confirmation CTA.

2. src/components/mission/MissionDetailPage.tsx
   Mission detail screen that leads into launch.
   Contains mission summary, timeline, status panels, and the “Launch mission” button.

3. src/components/mission/MissionSelectionPage.tsx
   Mission selection screen where users choose a mission before viewing details.

4. src/app/routes.tsx
   App routing definitions for mission pages, including the launch route and results route.

5. src/components/LaunchSequence.jsx
   Launch state component for countdown / launch / complete sequence UI.

6. src/components/MissionVisualizationFallback.jsx
   Fallback visualization of spacecraft movement and mission path.

7. src/App.tsx
   Root app wrapper mounting the application and routes.

8. src/components/results/MissionResultsPage.tsx
   Results page reached after confirming launch.

9. src/data/missions.ts
   Mission metadata used to populate mission details and launch screen content.

10. src/components/landing/LandingPage.tsx
   Landing page entry point into the broader app experience.
